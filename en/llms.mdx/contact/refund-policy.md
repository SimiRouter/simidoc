# Refund Policy

Thank you for using SimiRouter. If you need to request a refund, please read the following information carefully.

## I. Eligibility

An account may request a refund if all of the following conditions are met\:

1. The request is made by the account owner;
2. The top-up was made no more than 120 days ago;
3. The account has no record of prohibited use;
4. The account still has an unused, refundable cash principal balance.

The platform may pause a refund review or reject a refund if the account has engaged in API abuse, attacks on the platform, fabricated transactions, fraudulent reward collection, unauthorized resale, or similar violations.

## II. Calculating the Refund

Refunds cover only the unused portion of the cash principal you actually paid.

The calculation is\:

**Refund amount = Total amount actually paid − Principal consumed**

Definitions\:

* **Total amount actually paid**\: The cash paid for your top-up, excluding bonus credits, promotional rewards, referral rewards, points deductions, and other non-cash amounts.
* **Principal consumed**\: The cash cost of your actual API usage, calculated using the discount applicable when you topped up.
* **Bonus credits, promotional rewards, referral rewards, points deductions, and similar amounts cannot be withdrawn or refunded.**

### Example 1\: Top-up without a Discount

You add 100 CNY in credit, pay 100 CNY, and use 50 CNY in credit\:

**Refund amount = 100 − 50 = 50 CNY**

### Example 2\: Discounted Top-up

You add 100 CNY in credit, pay 80 CNY (a price factor of 0.8), and use 50 CNY in credit\:

Principal consumed = 50 × 0.8 = 40 CNY

Refund amount = 80 − 40 = 40 CNY

### Example 3\: Multiple Top-ups

If you have multiple top-up records, the platform calculates the refund using the payment dates, credited amounts, amounts actually paid, applicable discounts, and usage records.

The final refund amount is subject to backend consumption records and the financial review.

## III. Processing Fees

The platform does not charge an additional processing fee for a standard refund.

Your refund is calculated using the amount actually paid, credit consumed, top-up discount, and remaining refundable principal.

If any of the following exceptional circumstances creates additional fees, the fees are charged at the actual amount incurred and confirmed with you before the refund\:

1. The original payment channel cannot process a refund to the original payment method;
2. You request a refund to a different payment account;
3. A bank transfer, cross-border transfer, or third-party account transfer incurs actual fees;
4. Incorrect recipient information or a returned payment requiring another transfer incurs additional fees.

Apart from these exceptions, no additional processing fee is deducted from a standard refund.

## IV. Request Process

1. Sign in to the console and contact online support.
2. Submit the information requested by support.
3. Support will accept the request within 24 hours and notify you that it is under review.
4. Review and refund processing usually take 3-7 business days.
5. You will receive a platform notification when the refund is complete.

## V. Required Information

To protect your account and funds, provide\:

1. **Identity verification information**
   Follow support's instructions to provide your registered email address, UID, a screenshot showing your current signed-in status, or a specified verification email sent from your registered email address, as needed to establish that you own the account.
2. **Reason for the refund**
   Briefly explain why you are requesting a refund.
3. **Recipient account information**
   Refunds are sent through the original payment channel whenever possible. If that is not possible, provide an Alipay, WeChat Pay, bank account, or other recipient details as requested by support.

Make sure all information is true, accurate, and complete. You are responsible for refund failures, delays, or additional costs caused by incorrect information.

## VI. Important Notes

1. Non-cash amounts, including bonus credits, promotional rewards, referral rewards, and points deductions, are not refundable.
2. Consumed credit is not refundable.
3. Accounts with no remaining balance, outstanding payments, or no refundable cash principal are not eligible for a refund.
4. An account with more than 2 refunds in total, or total refunds exceeding 2000 CNY, requires an additional manual review.
5. The platform may pause a refund review if it finds abnormal top-ups, abnormal usage, malicious refunds, or fraudulent reward collection.
6. If you have questions about the refund amount, contact support to request a further review within 7 days of receiving the refund result.

## VII. Contact Us

Support hours\: Daily, 9\:00 - 21\:00

Support channel\: Sign in to the console and submit your refund request through online support.

This policy applies to refunds for SimiRouter services. If platform rules change, the latest announcement or support explanation takes precedence.
