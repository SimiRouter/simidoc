# Referral Rebate Policy

## Commission rules

### Confirm the referral relationship

* The referred user must register with the referrer’s link; otherwise the relationship cannot be confirmed.
* In the console’s [Referral Information](https://api.dwchainless.com/promotion), referrers can view users registered through their link, relationships, and commission records.

### Commission

* After a successful recharge by a referred user, the referrer receives 10% of that recharge as commission.

### Withdrawals

* Referrers can view commission and request withdrawal from referral details.
  * The withdrawal amount must be at least CNY 1,000.
* Withdrawal requests require platform approval before payment.
  * First-time applicants must provide receiving details, such as Alipay, WeChat, or a bank account.
* Commission for newly referred users\:
  * To prevent fraud, commission from a new user’s first recharge is reviewed. It is granted after normal behavior is confirmed. See the risk controls and penalties below.

### Commission rate

* The standard rate is 10% of the referred user’s recharge.
* Users may apply to become platform partners for a higher rate, determined by user level and referral count. Contact support to apply.

## Risk controls and penalties

### Purpose of review

Protect users from malicious attacks and preserve normal access and lawful rights.

Maintain a fair and usable platform.

Prevent platform losses and protect its economic interests.

Comply with law and regulatory obligations.

### Scope of review

Malicious attacks that damage the platform through technical or other means.

Reward farming through technical or rule loopholes.

Unauthorized promotion, impersonation, false claims, promises of extra commission, or misleading registration campaigns.

Market disruption, including trading platform rewards contrary to its rules.

Using platform products for unlawful activity.

### Review process

First-recharge commission is marked Pending review and frozen for 7 calendar days. Data analysis and manual checks assess abnormal behavior during that period.

After review, the referrer can see the result in referral details. Normal commission is released to withdrawable balance; abnormal commission is cancelled and may trigger further penalties.

A referrer may appeal within 7 business days of notice by submitting evidence such as chat logs or proof of a genuine relationship. The platform reviews and replies within 5 business days.

### Penalties

|           Risk behavior           | Risk level |                            Penalties                           |
| :-------------------------------: | :--------: | :------------------------------------------------------------: |
|          Malicious attack         |    High    |           Immediate permanent account and service ban          |
|            Reward fraud           |   Medium   | Recover improper gains; serious cases result in an account ban |
|       Unauthorized promotion      |   Medium   |             Serious cases result in an account ban             |
|         Market disruption         |   Medium   |    Financial penalty; serious cases result in an account ban   |
| Unlawful use of platform products |    High    |           Immediate permanent account and service ban          |

### Violations

* Bulk registration of multiple accounts by one party;
* Self-referral or self-funded commission farming;
* False identity or payment details;
* Fabricated transactions or recharges, or malicious refunds;
* Bulk reward farming through scripts or automation;
* Exploiting system defects, promotion errors, or API faults;
* Manipulating, trading, or maliciously transferring referrals;
* Unauthorized top-ups, money laundering, stolen payments, or other unlawful participation;
* Organizing or assisting malicious arbitrage;
* Other breaches of fairness, good faith, user terms, or law.
