# Long-term Referral Guide

## Referral Policy

### Ongoing Rebates for Valid Referral Relationships

When the referral relationship remains valid, both accounts are in good standing, and the platform’s current rules are met, a referred user’s subsequent eligible top-ups may generate rebates under the applicable rules.

### More Referrals, More Rewards

More eligible top-ups by qualifying referred users can generate more rebates. Eligibility, rates, and review requirements follow the [Referral Rebate Policy](/simidoc/en/promotion/rebate-details) and current console information.

### Partner Program

If you are interested and can demonstrate your referral capabilities, you may be offered an upgrade to partner status with higher referral earnings.

## How to Refer Users

Getting started takes three steps\:

1. **Get your referral link**\: Sign in to the console, open [Referral Information](https://api.dwchainless.com/promotion), and copy your personal referral link.
2. **Register through the link**\: Share your personal link with the person you wish to refer. They must complete registration through that link to establish the referral relationship.
3. **Check relationships and records**\: In Referral Information, review the linked users and rebate records to check whether eligible subsequent top-ups generated the corresponding rebates.

<Callout type="info" title="">
  Referral relationships, rebate amounts, and review status are determined by actual console records. If a record does not match your expectations, contact support with your account and relevant order information.
</Callout>
