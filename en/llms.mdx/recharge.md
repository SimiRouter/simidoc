# Top-ups

## 1. Check your wallet

After registering and signing in, open Wallet in the console to review your balance, available plans, and order history.

[Open Wallet](https://api.dwchainless.com/wallet)

## 2. Choose a plan and add credit

Before choosing a plan, check the **payment amount, credited quota, validity period, and eligibility conditions**. Plans may change; the current Wallet and payment confirmation pages take precedence. The amount you pay is not the same as your usable quota, and a top-up does not grant access to every model.

![Current Wallet trial-plan example showing payment amount, credited quota, and validity](/simidoc/assets/start/wallet-plan-2026-10.jpg)

*Captured from the local deployment on 2026-10-22 to illustrate the interface, not to promise pricing. Select the image to view the original.*

Choose a suitable plan, select an available option under Payment Method, review the order, and follow the payment instructions. Do not pay twice.

<Callout type="info" title="">
  If no payment method is available, or top-ups or redemption are not enabled, contact the administrator instead of repeatedly submitting.
</Callout>

## 3. Redeem a code (optional)

If you have a valid redemption code, enter it in the Wallet redemption section. Codes are generally single-use; availability, expiry, and credited quota depend on the result returned by the page.

[Refund Policy](/simidoc/en/contact/refund-policy)
