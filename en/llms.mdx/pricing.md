# Pricing

## View current model prices

Open [Model Square](https://api.dwchainless.com/en/pricing/), then select a model and group to inspect current pricing. Input, output, cache, and per-request charges may differ; the actual page and your group permissions take precedence.

![Model pricing page\: after selecting a pricing group, the table lists model ID, spec, and price](/simidoc/assets/guide/pricing-2026-11.png)

*Screenshot from the local deployment, updated 2026-11-17. It shows where the page sits, not a price commitment. Tap the image to open it full size.*

A top-up amount or credited quota is not the model price itself. Start with a small request and check the actual charge in [Usage Logs](https://api.dwchainless.com/usage-logs/common).
