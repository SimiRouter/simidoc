Three launch benefits

Rules for the SimiRouter launch campaign: recharge rebates, referral rebates, and traffic bonuses, including stacking and payout details.

Historical promotion

The promotion described here ended at 2026-08-10 00:00:00 (Beijing time). This page is retained for historical order rules. Any extension or new promotion requires a new platform announcement; this page does not extend it automatically.

The following are the original launch promotion rules.

To celebrate the official launch of SimiRouter, the platform offered recharge bonuses, referral commission, referral quota rewards as three benefits.

  • recharge bonuses: receive an additional 10%–20% of credited recharge value in quota redemption codes.
  • referral commission: standard referral recharge commission is 10%, increased to 20% during the promotion.
  • referral quota rewards: referrers receive quota codes worth 50% of a referred user’s eligible credited recharge value.

During the promotion, eligible user recharges and referred-user recharges qualify for the corresponding benefits. All three can apply together when their conditions are satisfied.

1. Promotion period

Start: when this announcement was published and the promotion went live
End: August 10, 2026 at 00:00:00

All times are Beijing time.

Eligibility and rates use the time when an order is successfully paid and its recharge principal is credited.

August 10, 2026 at 00:00:00 onward:

  • Referral recharge commission returns from 20% to the standard 10%
  • The 10%–20% recharge redemption-code bonus ends
  • The 50% referral quota-code promotion ends
  • Existing valid referral relationships remain; subsequent eligible recharges use the standard 10% commission

Extensions or changes are governed by the latest SimiRouter announcement.

2. Eligibility

All registered SimiRouter users with accounts in good standing may participate.

Referral reward participants must also meet these conditions:

  1. The referred user registers or binds through the referrer’s unique link, code, or another approved method
  2. The relationship must be established before the referred user recharges
  3. SimiRouter system records determine the referral relationship
  4. Each account can be bound to only one referrer
  5. Once established, the relationship generally cannot be changed or bound again by the user
  6. Existing referred users making eligible recharges during the promotion also qualify
  7. Orders with no valid relationship, codes added after recharge, or retrospective binding requests do not qualify

3. Definitions and calculation basis

1. Eligible credited recharge amount

An eligible credited recharge is the amount paid through a supported payment channel and successfully credited as recharge principal.

The following do not count:

  • Recharge bonus amounts
  • Redemption-code credits
  • Referral commission
  • Platform compensation quota
  • Promotional quota
  • Test quota
  • Refunded, cancelled, or disputed order amounts
  • Any other balance or quota not obtained by the user’s actual payment

2. Referral commission

After a referred user completes an eligible recharge, the platform pays the referrer a commission calculated as a percentage of that credited amount.

SimiRouter settlement records determine the actual amount.

3. Redemption-code rewards

These are SimiRouter quota redemption codes issued under the promotion rules.

Redeemed quota can only pay for supported AI models and API services on SimiRouter. It cannot be withdrawn or exchanged for cash. Expiration, eligible uses, and restrictions follow the code page or issuance notice.

4. Benefit 1: up to 20% referral recharge commission

After a valid referral is established, each eligible recharge by the referred user earns commission for the referrer.

Promotional commission rate

For eligible recharges during the promotion, the referrer receives a 20% referral commission.

Example:
A referred user has an eligible credited recharge of CNY 100; the referrer receives:
100 × 20% = CNY 20 commission

Rate after the promotion

The referral relationship remains after the promotion. Subsequent eligible recharges earn the referrer the standard 10% commission.

Example:
After the promotion, a referred user has an eligible credited recharge of CNY 100; the referrer receives:
100 × 10% = CNY 10 commission

The standard 10% commission continues while the relationship is valid, both accounts remain in good standing, and no violations occur.

5. Benefit 2: an additional 10%–20% recharge quota bonus

During the promotion, eligible recharges receive normal principal credit plus redemption codes worth 10%–20% of that credited amount.

The rate must be clearly shown on the recharge page or tier before payment.

Example:
A user has an eligible credited recharge during the promotion of CNY 100 and receives additional SimiRouter redemption codes worth CNY 10–20.

That is:

  • A 10% bonus gives a CNY 10 code
  • A 15% bonus gives a CNY 15 code
  • A 20% bonus gives a CNY 20 code

The code is an additional gift and does not reduce the original recharge credit.

6. Benefit 3: another 50% quota reward for referral recharges

During the promotion, the referrer receives benefit 1’s 20% commission plus redemption codes worth 50% of the eligible credited recharge.

Example:
A referred user has an eligible credited recharge of CNY 100; the referrer additionally receives:
100 × 50% = a CNY 50 redemption code

This code reward and commission are calculated separately and can both be received.

More valid referrals and eligible recharges produce more code rewards for the referrer.

7. Combined benefits example

User A successfully invites user B to register and establishes the referral relationship.

User B has an eligible credited recharge during the promotion of CNY 100, resulting in:

User B receives

  • Normal principal credit: CNY 100
  • Benefit 2: redemption codes worth CNY 10–20

User A receives

  • Benefit 1 commission: CNY 20
  • Benefit 3 quota code: CNY 50

The three rewards are calculated independently without offsetting each other.

User A’s referral rewards do not affect user B’s recharge credit or bonus.

8. Reward distribution

  1. After an eligible recharge, the system calculates rewards from order status, credited value, and referral relationship
  2. Commission settlement follows records shown in Referral information, Wallet, or the commission page
  3. Codes are distributed through the redemption page, platform messages, or another designated method
  4. Distribution may be delayed by payment synchronization, risk review, or system delays
  5. If a successful recharge’s reward remains missing, contact SimiRouter support with account information and the order number
  6. Rewards use the system’s settlement precision; final settlement governs rounding differences
  7. Redeem codes before expiration; unused expired codes follow their displayed rules
  8. Users bear losses caused by incorrect account details, wrong referral binding, leaked codes, or poor account security
  9. Withdrawal availability, thresholds, fees, and payment times follow the Wallet or referral page rules

9. Combining promotions

  1. Benefits 1, 2, and 3 can apply together when all conditions are met
  2. Combining this promotion with others depends on those promotions’ specific terms
  3. Do not split orders, submit repeatedly, or fabricate payments to collect duplicate rewards
  4. Duplicate rewards issued by system error may be reversed or deducted after verification
  5. Spending redemption credits, bonus quota, or commission does not count as a new eligible recharge or earn rewards again

10. Exclusions

Orders or accounts are excluded in any of these cases:

  1. Payment did not succeed
  2. The recharge was not actually credited
  3. Credit arrived before the promotion began or after it ended
  4. The order was refunded, cancelled, charged back, or disputed
  5. Credits obtained through codes, gifts, compensation, or commission rather than cash payment
  6. No valid referral binding, or binding completed after recharge
  7. Fabricated, modified, or transferred referrals through unapproved methods
  8. Test orders, internal debugging, or orders explicitly excluded by the platform
  9. Accounts that are restricted, frozen, banned, closed, or have unresolved security or compliance issues
  10. Other failures to meet these rules

11. Violations and abnormal activity

To preserve fairness, obtaining rewards improperly is prohibited, including:

  • Bulk registration of accounts by one party
  • Self-referral or recharging to earn one’s own referral rewards
  • False identity or payment information
  • Fabricated transactions or recharges, or malicious refunds
  • Bulk reward farming with scripts or automation
  • Exploiting system defects, promotion configuration errors, or API faults
  • Manipulating, trading, or maliciously transferring referral relationships
  • Participating through unauthorized top-ups, money laundering, stolen payments, or other unlawful means
  • Organizing or assisting malicious arbitrage
  • Other conduct contrary to fairness, good faith, user terms, or law

SimiRouter may delay rewards for suspicious orders or accounts while investigating.

For verified violations, SimiRouter may take one or more appropriate measures:

  1. Disqualify participation
  2. Withhold rewards
  3. Reverse, freeze, or deduct issued rewards
  4. Restrict recharge, redemption, withdrawal, or related account functions
  5. Suspend or permanently ban accounts
  6. Require return of improperly obtained rewards
  7. Report suspected offences to authorities or pursue legal responsibility

Users disputing a decision may submit order records, payment evidence, and other relevant materials to support for review.

12. Refunds and cancellations

  1. A full refund cancels all commission, recharge bonuses, and referral codes generated by that order
  2. A partial refund recalculates rewards from the remaining eligible recharge amount
  3. Issued or used rewards may be recovered from balances, commission, or future rewards
  4. If balances are insufficient, related functions may be restricted until the abnormal order is resolved
  5. Orders invalidated by chargebacks, stolen-payment complaints, or other disputes are treated as refunded orders

13. System incidents and force majeure

SimiRouter may adjust, suspend, or terminate the promotion within applicable law if it cannot operate normally because of:

  • Network or server failures, or maintenance
  • Payment-channel incidents
  • Third-party service interruptions
  • Hacking, viruses, malware, or security incidents
  • Natural disasters, war, major public events, or other force majeure
  • Changes in laws, regulatory policy, or industry rules
  • Other circumstances beyond reasonable platform control

Material rule changes will be announced through platform notices, promotion pages, or other appropriate means.

Verified eligible orders completed before a rule change generally follow the rules effective when the order occurred.

14. Other provisions

  1. Participation constitutes acknowledgment and acceptance of these rules
  2. Users must also follow SimiRouter’s user agreement, privacy policy, recharge rules, and other platform rules
  3. Amounts shown are calculation examples only; actual rewards follow system settlement
  4. The promotion is not a guarantee of principal, income, or investment returns
  5. Users must report and pay taxes on referral commission as required by local law
  6. Within applicable law, SimiRouter may reasonably adjust rules for operational, risk, or regulatory reasons and announce changes
  7. Unspecified matters are handled by the operator under fairness, good faith, and applicable law
  8. Applicable law prevails over any conflicting provision

15. Interpretation

Within applicable law, final interpretation belongs to the SimiRouter operator. This does not exclude or limit users’ statutory rights.
